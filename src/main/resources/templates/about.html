About Rizq

Share Food. Spread Hope. Share Happiness.

Every day, restaurants, bakeries and households in Bangladesh throw away perfectly good food while millions go hungry. Rizq closes that gap with a simple bridge between people who have surplus and organisations that can distribute it.

We operate in Dhaka, Chattogram and Sylhet with verified partners including BRAC Humanitarian, Bidyanondo Foundation, JAAGO Foundation and As-Sunnah Foundation.

Our Mission

Zero edible food wasted, zero person left hungry.

Our Values

Dignity, transparency and speed in every pickup.

Our Impact

Thousands of meals rescued and delivered every month.
