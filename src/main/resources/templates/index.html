How It Works

Three simple steps from surplus to smile.

1. List Your Food

Donors post surplus meals, bakery items or groceries with pickup time and location.

2. NGO Claims It

Verified NGOs nearby see the listing instantly and claim what they can distribute.

3. Delivered With Care

The NGO picks up, distributes, and marks the donation delivered — you see the impact.
